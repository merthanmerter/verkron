# Vanta: AI-Driven Investment Research for Borsa İstanbul

[Vanta](https://vanta.verkron.com) · AI & Quantitative Research Platform

## The Opportunity

Picking stocks well takes what a professional buy-side desk has: fresh data,
disciplined multi-signal analysis, and a committee that challenges its own
conclusions. Vanta packages that workflow into a single tool — every week it
scans the entire Borsa İstanbul market and publishes a portfolio of exactly
four stocks, each backed by written evidence.

## How It Works

- **Live discovery** — candidates are pulled from the whole exchange each run;
  nothing is hardcoded, and stale data fails loudly rather than silently.
- **Multi-signal scoring** — news flow, fundamentals, technicals, value-quality,
  liquidity and risk are scored deterministically and blended transparently.
- **News intelligence** — targeted Turkish-language news search per company,
  with global investment-bank commentary on BIST treated as a first-class signal.
- **AI committee** — several reasoning models independently assess the finalists,
  then their views are challenged and synthesized into one verdict. The AI writes
  the reasoning; it never invents the numbers.
- **Evidence-first reporting** — every holding ships with its bull case, bear
  case, catalysts, and risks in a shareable weekly report.

## Built for Trust

Selection is fully deterministic and auditable — the same data always yields the
same portfolio, and every score can be reconstructed from its components. The
system verifies itself: each pipeline stage reports what it fetched, how fresh
it is, and which models actually ran.

## The Impact

Vanta turns a weekend of manual research into a checkpointed pipeline that runs
in about a minute, and its weekly reports are published at
[vanta.verkron.com](https://vanta.verkron.com).
